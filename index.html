How to use this tool

This self-assessment covers all 20 Effortful Thinking Habits across four domains. For each habit, you will be asked how consistently you use it with intentionality — not just whether you do it, but whether you do it deliberately and name it as part of your instruction.

There are no right answers. The goal is to surface your current strengths and identify one or two habits to explore next. Your results are private and not recorded anywhere.
